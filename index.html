About Us

Welcome to Lash Wonderland. Lash Wonderland is a full service professional eyelash extension studio. We accommodate each eyelash individually and our technicians have many years of experience in New York City. We guarantee that we only provide the finest product in the industry. We work hard to ensure that all of our clients are completely satisfied. We specialize in making people feel confident and look beautiful. Every girl is a sleeping beauty; Lash Wonderland does only one thing, to wake you up!

Upon your appointment at Lash Wonderland, you will meet one of our experts and we will customize your lashes before the procedure. We will show you different type of lashes, curls, thicknesses, lengths and designs to figure out the best look for your eyes and achieve the look you desire. The procedure is relaxing enough to fall asleep and it may take about an hour to an hour thirty minutes depending on different service received.
